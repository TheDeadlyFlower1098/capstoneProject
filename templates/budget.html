How Transactions Work:

When you update your Saved amount, PlanIT automatically calculates the difference between your previous balance and your new one.
  • If your saved amount increases, it is recorded as a positive transaction.
    Example: $300 → $400 = +$100
  • If your saved amount decreases, it is recorded as a negative transaction (a purchase).
    Example: $300 → $150 = −$150
Before saving, you will be asked what the transaction was for (e.g., "groceries", "gas", etc.). This note will appear in your transaction history below.
